On Tuesday 2nd September, I joined the group 11. The subsequent day, I met with the team members after the Software Architecture lecture to discuss and think about the project, choosing a system we would like to develop.

I spent around 2 hours in the preliminary meeting bouncing around ideas with the team, deciding which industry sector we would like to work on.
