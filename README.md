# TravelGo - Software Architecture

We all love to travel! Seeing new places, learning about the culture and just tasting the finest of cuisines is what makes holidays so worth it! On the other hand, sometimes we do not have the luxury to dedicate as much time to our visiting sights as we might like. So, picture this: an interactive platform that enhances your cultural knowledge about the attractions that are on your bucket-list.

Tick every attraction on your bucket-list in a fun competitive way with other tourists exploring the sites. Learn, test your knowledge and earn brownie points as you progress on your trip. Redeem the points to receive discounts on entry fees to the attractions or earn a free souvenir. Moreover, you can compete against other tourists, top the leaderboard and be crowned the ultimate traveller! This platform aims to make traveling more fun with short side-quests and enable tourists to meet like-minded individuals on their trips.
